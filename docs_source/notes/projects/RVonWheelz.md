# RVonWheelz — community car and bike pooling (personal project)

Written by Prashobh Paul for the Knowledge Fabric, November 2026.

## What it is
The very first thing I built for people other than myself. I live in Raheja Vistas Elite,
Nacharam, in Hyderabad, where the daily office commute was a shared headache that
affected me too. On RVonWheelz neighbours offer or book car and bike rides, chat with
co-riders, and get real-time alerts for seat requests, approvals and reminders. It is a
small, non-profit solution to a very local problem.

## Technology
React and TypeScript with Capacitor for Android, and Supabase for data and real-time
updates. A companion RideAnalyticsDashboard (HTML, August 2026) shows how the community
uses it.
