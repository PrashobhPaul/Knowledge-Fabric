# Knowledge Fabric and Knowledge Factory (personal project)

Written by Prashobh Paul for the Knowledge Fabric, November 2026.

## What it is
My demonstration of explainable, citation-grounded AI, and the project I am most attached
to right now. It is not just agentic AI but a knowledge layer: the same pattern I want for
the AI-driven development life cycle at my company.

## How it works
The Knowledge Factory syncs everything public about me: my website, resume, GitHub,
YouTube channel and LinkedIn. The Knowledge Fabric links it into one typed knowledge graph
and answers questions with verbatim, cited evidence, a derived confidence score and the
lineage of every sentence. Curated answers in my own words sit on top, each with proofs.
Sentence embeddings let it understand questions by meaning, and a feedback loop turns
flagged answers into curator work. It answers in English, Malayalam, Telugu and Hindi, and
a 3D avatar speaks the answers.

## Technology
Python for the Factory and the indexing pipeline (BM25, LSA and MiniLM sentence
embeddings, a typed graph), and a fully static JavaScript site: no backend, no vector
database and no API keys.
