# Koode — journey safety and wellbeing (personal project)

Written by Prashobh Paul for the Knowledge Fabric, November 2026.

## What it is
Koode is an Android app for the people who worry when you travel. The name is
Malayalam for "together" or "with you". It lets family and friends know you are okay
on a journey without you having to call or text.

## Why I built it
My parents still worry every time I drive between Hyderabad and Thrissur, so I built
Koode to give them calm, live reassurance instead of phone calls at every stop.

## How it works
Koode does not track a person; it watches the journey. It rates the journey's health as
Normal, Attention or Concern, and gives loved ones a reassurance channel with a live map,
an ETA and a timeline of the trip.

## Privacy by design
Koode is invitation-only: each follower is approved by name. After arrival it builds a verified journey report; followers keep access for an hour, then the shared copy is gone.

## Technology
Kotlin with Jetpack Compose on Android, OpenStreetMap for maps and Supabase for live
sharing. It is fully open source and zero cost: no Google services and no billing anywhere.
