# Soccer.AI — an AI companion for the 2026 FIFA World Cup (personal project)

Written by Prashobh Paul for the Knowledge Fabric, November 2026.

## What it is
My most recent build and the one I am still actively tinkering with: an AI companion for
the 2026 World Cup with AI-written match stories, tournament simulations and visual
analytics, and a live Ballon d'Or prediction.

## Technology
An offline-first progressive web app in React and TypeScript with Supabase, installable on
a phone like a native app.
