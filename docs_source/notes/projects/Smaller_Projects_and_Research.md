Written by Prashobh Paul for the Knowledge Fabric, November 2026.

- Dhinasuthra (Kotlin, August 2026): an Android app to capture and identify the patterns
  in your daily routine.
- Finance 10-K Automation (2023): automating the download of 10-K and 10-Q filings and
  applying NLP to them.
- OpenNLP NER tagging model (2018): from my TCS years, when I built custom OpenNLP models
  and an intelligent date tagger.
- Chatbot (2016): one of my earliest conversational AI experiments.
- Digital Card (2025 to 2026): my digital business card and the earlier website chatbot
  that this knowledge fabric is meant to replace.
- SDLC Lifecycle Kiro Automation (2026): experiments with Kiro for agentic SDLC
  automation.

## Where it all started
My voice-controlled UAV, a climate-analyser smart plane I flew by voice on Engineer's Day
in 2012 using Hidden Markov Model based speech recognition, which made the regional Telugu
newspapers and became my launchpad into AI.
