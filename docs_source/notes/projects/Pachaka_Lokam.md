# Pachaka Lokam — offline South-Indian meal planner (personal project)

Written by Prashobh Paul for the Knowledge Fabric, November 2026.

## What it is
Pachaka Lokam, Malayalam for "the world of cooking", is my most personal project: a
100% offline South-Indian meal planner.

## Why I built it
It started as a hobby to make life easier for my mom and my wife in the kitchen, and to
make sure I buy everything the kitchen needs during a busy day.

## How it works
It suggests today's meals from what is actually in your kitchen, remembers festival
menus, and tracks grocery and household reminders.

## Privacy
No accounts, no cloud and no tracking. Everything stays on the phone.

## Technology
A JavaScript progressive web app, offline-first, installable on Android from the browser
with Add to Home screen.
