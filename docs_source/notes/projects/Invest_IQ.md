# Invest.IQ — AI stock research for Indian equities (personal project)

Written by Prashobh Paul for the Knowledge Fabric, November 2026.

## What it is
Invest.IQ is where I pushed AI the furthest in my personal work: agentic AI applied to
the Indian stock market. Heavy analytics and an AI reasoning layer turn a tiered universe
of Indian equities into one opinionated, backtest-first shortlist across short, medium and
long horizons.

## How it works
Quality scores, technicals and news influence are all weighed, and explained, so you can
see exactly why each pick ranks where it does. It is my playground for real-world applied
AI. It is educational only, not financial advice.

## Lineage
Invest.IQ grew out of earlier experiments that are also public on GitHub:
StockAnalysis_IndianMarket (2026, Python, stock analysis using the O'Neil score,
technicals and news inputs, with FinBERT and transformers on NSE and BSE data) and
ProfitPilot (2025 to 2026, Python, stock prediction).

## Technology
React and TypeScript, packaged with Capacitor for Android, with an analytics and AI
reasoning layer.
