# IPLBuzz — IPL 2026 analytics dashboard (personal project)

Written by Prashobh Paul for the Knowledge Fabric, November 2026.

## What it is
One of my favourite sports side projects. IPLBuzz keeps the whole IPL 2026 season in one
place: points tables, squads, batting and bowling leaderboards, live scores and match
analytics. What started as a simple season tracker grew into a complete cricket analytics
dashboard. Cricket is my favourite sport and CSK is my IPL team.

## Technology
A JavaScript and HTML web app with analytics dashboards.
