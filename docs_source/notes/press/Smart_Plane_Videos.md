## Titles and dates, synced by the Knowledge Factory

When this note was written, YouTube could not be reached, so the YouTube titles, the upload
dates and the uploader's channel name were not on record. The Knowledge Factory reads them on its
weekly sync in GitHub Actions: the channel through its public RSS feed, after looking up its
channel id from the @Phoenix007Super page, and the video through YouTube's oEmbed endpoint and
its watch page. The results go to knowledge_sources/youtube/extra_videos.json. Until the first
sync reaches YouTube, the records there carry the placeholder titles "Phoenix007Super channel"
and "Smart plane demonstration (college upload)" and are marked pending_sync.
